This page lists every book of the Finkeissen Research Program with a one-line summary and direct links to the preview PDFs.

Preview rule: teasers cover page 1 up to the preface of Chapter 1; full volumes remain with the author (print/PDF).
Machine-readable details: one YAML manifest per book in [`books/`](books/){target=_blank}. Background: [books/README.md](books/README.md){target=_blank}.
